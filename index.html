The Broader Context
SAP SuccessFactors Employee Time Tracking
SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking enables organisations to plan and record workforce attendance, manage time-related information, and evaluate attendance patterns. It is a core component of SAP SuccessFactors HXM Core, implementing the Attendance Management business capability within the Time Management domain.

Attendance Recording

Comprehensive clock-in/clock-out tracking, time sheet submission, and absence recording for the entire workforce.

Calendar View

Visual monthly and weekly calendar layouts showing attendance status, leave balances, and pending regularisations.

Self-Service

Employees manage their own time data, reducing HR administrative overhead and increasing data accuracy.

Compliance

Ensure regulatory compliance with automated time evaluation, overtime tracking, and policy enforcement.

The Challenge
Why the Existing Workflow Falls Short

Employees need immediate visibility into their attendance status

The standard SuccessFactors homepage does not display the attendance calendar directly. Employees must navigate through multiple screens to check their attendance status, identify gaps, and regularise entries.

  • No direct calendar visibility on the homepage
  • Multiple navigation steps to reach attendance details
  • Employees often forget to regularise pending entries
  • Lack of proactive prompts leads to compliance gaps
  • HR teams spend cycles following up on missing records

Our Approach
1-Click Attendance Calendar View
Instead of embedding the full calendar on the homepage (which introduces performance and layout constraints), we deliver a smarter approach: a contextual prompt that guides employees to their attendance calendar in a single click.

Banner Card + Org Card on Homepage

A prominent, contextual banner card and org card placed on the SuccessFactors homepage that proactively prompts employees to review and regularise their attendance. One click opens the full calendar view with all pending items highlighted.
